# Community Conduct

We want technical discussion to be rigorous, direct, and respectful.

Participants should:
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people,
- distinguish evidence, assumptions, and opinion,
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted abuse,
- respect privacy and confidential information,
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing proposals.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct materially harms contributors or the project. Security issues should be reported through `SECURITY.md`, not public issue threads.
